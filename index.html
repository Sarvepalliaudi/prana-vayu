1. Ground Spotters NSS / NCC / Students

Volunteers who observe suspected micro-climate plumes (leaf burning, vehicle idling, road dust).

  • Record verified GPS location & visible conditions
  • Never approach fires or confront operators
  • Submit structured observations into PRANA-VAYU
2. Community Gatekeepers Principals / Wardens

Campus estate managers and administrators who review incoming triage reports.

  • Deploy 15-minute aqueous hose dousing on smoldering leaves
  • Reroute pedestrian corridors 120m upwind
  • Throttle ground-floor HVAC fresh air intakes
3. Statutory Regulators State PCBs / Municipalities

Authorized personnel who review draft administrative representations and record statutory action.

  • Section 19(5) Air (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1981
  • Section 31A statutory directions and penal sanctions
  • Formal double-bordered printable representation notices
